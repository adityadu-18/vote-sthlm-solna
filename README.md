Solna handles localized, immediate public services like primary/secondary education, preschools, social services, city planning, and elderly care.

Political Majority: Solna is governed by a historic four-party majority known as The Solna Coalition (Solnakoalitionen), led by Sara Kukka-Salam (Social Democrats) as the Mayor/Chair of the Municipal Executive Board. [1]

Governing Parties: A cross-bloc majority consisting of the Social Democrats (S), Left Party (V), Green Party (MP), and the Centre Party (C). This coalition formed after the 2022 local elections, unseating the Moderate Party which had ruled Solna for 24 continuous years. [1]

Opposition: The center-right bloc, composed of the Moderate Party (M), Liberal Party (L), Christian Democrats (KD), and Sweden Democrats (SD). [1]

Regional Governance: Stockholm Region (Region Stockholm)

The Stockholm Region is a much larger administrative tier overseeing the entire Stockholm County (consisting of 26 municipalities, including Solna). Its primary and most resource-heavy mandates are healthcare (all hospitals and public clinics) and public transport (SL trains, buses, and subways). [1, 2]

Political Majority: Governed by a minority coalition that functions as a majority with structural voting support from a fourth partner. The administration is led by Aida Hadzialic (Social Democrats) as the Finance Regional Councilor. [1, 2]

Governing Parties: The formal coalition includes the Social Democrats (S)

, Centre Party (C), and Green Party (MP). They rely on a formal partnership with the Left Party (V) to clear the 75-seat majority voting threshold in the 149-seat Regional Council. [1, 2, 3]

Opposition: The center-right opposition consists of the Moderate Party (M), Sweden Democrats (SD), Christian Democrats (KD), and Liberals (L). [1]
